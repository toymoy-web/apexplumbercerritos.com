About The District.

What Cerritos Towne Center Is.

Cerritos Towne Center is a mixed retail, office, and entertainment district in the center of Cerritos, built on about 125 acres of former dairy farms. The formal planning area is bounded by State Route 91 on the north, Shoemaker Avenue on the east, 183rd Street on the south, and Bloomfield Avenue on the west. Local press once nicknamed it the Golden Triangle.

The first phase opened in 1990, and the Cerritos Center for the Performing Arts opened in January 1993. City Hall, the Cerritos Sheriff Station, the Cerritos Millennium Library, and Cerritos High School sit across the street and are sometimes called Downtown Cerritos. This page is about businesses in the district. If you need help at home, see our main Cerritos page.

Commercial Plumbing Needs.

What Businesses In A District Like This Call About.

Restaurants And Food Service

Commercial kitchens send grease and food waste into drain lines. Grease interceptors need regular pumping, and a backed-up line can force a kitchen to close until it is cleared.

Backflow Prevention

Water suppliers in California commonly require backflow assemblies on commercial water service and irrigation to be tested by a certified tester. California's cross-connection control rules in Title 17 of the California Code of Regulations set the testing requirements, and your water supplier applies them to your property.

Restrooms And Fixtures

Public restrooms see steady use all day. Running toilets, leaking flush valves, and slow drains waste water and can lead to closures if they are left alone.

Hot Water

Offices, hotels, and restaurants rely on steady hot water. A failed water heater affects customers and staff, so tell us the unit type and size when you call.

If you lease your space, ask your landlord or property manager before major plumbing work. Many leases split responsibility between building lines and tenant improvements, so the answer depends on what part of the system is failing.

For after-hours problems, shut off the water first and protect equipment and stock if it is safe to do so. Then call our 24/7 line and tell us whether the leak is near electrical panels or machines. Commercial plumbing work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and your city decides when a permit is needed.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

Shut off the water at the nearest valve if you can reach it safely. Then call our 24/7 line and tell us if the leak is near electrical equipment.

FAQs

Questions From Towne Center Businesses.

Is emergency service available in Cerritos Towne Center?

Our emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends, and holidays. Call (562) 488-9614 for a burst pipe, an active leak, or a sewage backup.

Do you handle commercial plumbing in the Towne Center?

Yes. We take commercial plumbing calls in Cerritos for restaurants, offices, and retail spaces. Call with your business type and a description of the problem.

Should I call my landlord or you first?

If you lease your space, check your lease. Many leases ask the tenant to notify the landlord or property manager, and in an emergency you can shut off the water and call both.

Can you test backflow assemblies?

Yes, see our backflow testing page. Your water supplier sets the testing rules and the schedule for your property.

How do I know if my business is inside the Towne Center area?

The planning area runs between State Route 91, Shoemaker Avenue, 183rd Street, and Bloomfield Avenue. Call with your address. We serve businesses across Cerritos either way.
