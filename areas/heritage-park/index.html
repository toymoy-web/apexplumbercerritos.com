About The Area.

Where The Heritage Park Area Is.

Heritage Park is a 15-acre city park near the center of Cerritos, close to Cerritos High School, the Cerritos Library, and the Towne Center. It is known for a colonial-themed play island, a moat with ducks and turtles, and a Paul Revere statue at the entrance. The city refurbished the park in 2002, and a neighborhood-data site also uses Heritage Park as a label for this part of central Cerritos.

There is no official boundary for the area, so we use the park, Cerritos High School, and the Towne Center as anchors. The same data site describes the surrounding area as a mix of single-family homes and apartment complexes, with many buildings from 1970 to 1999 and both owners and renters. We treat that as a general description, not a statement about any one address.

Owners, Renters, And Apartments.

How A Plumbing Call Works In Each Case.

If You Own Your Home

You decide on repairs and choose the plumber. Shut off the water at the main or at the fixture valve, then call us and describe what you can see.

If You Rent

Your lease usually says who handles plumbing repairs. Many leases ask you to tell the landlord or property manager first, so check yours before you hire anyone. California Civil Code Section 1941.1 treats working plumbing as part of a habitable rental, which is one reason repairs usually go through the landlord.

If You Live In An Apartment

Drain lines in a multi-unit building are often shared. If more than one unit has slow drains, tell the property manager so the main line can be checked.

Outdoor Water Lines

Hose bibs and irrigation lines can leak without anyone noticing. A lawn that stays soggy, or a water meter that moves with everything off, is worth a call.

Slow drains in several fixtures at once usually point to a clog in a larger line, not a problem at one sink. A single slow sink is more often a local clog. Telling us which one you have helps us bring the right equipment.

If you are not sure whether your street counts as the Heritage Park area, it does not matter for service. We serve all of Cerritos, and we only need your street address to confirm the location. Plumbing repairs follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

Shut off the water at the nearest valve if you can reach it safely. Then call our 24/7 line, and tell your landlord or property manager if you rent.

FAQs

Questions From Heritage Park Residents.

Where is the Heritage Park area of Cerritos?

Heritage Park is a 15-acre park near the city center, close to Cerritos High School, the Cerritos Library, and the Towne Center. The area has no official boundary, so call us with your address and we will confirm we serve it.

Is emergency service available near Heritage Park?

Our emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends, and holidays. Call (562) 488-9614 for a burst pipe, an active leak, or a sewage backup.

I rent near Heritage Park. Should I call you or my landlord?

Check your lease. Many leases ask you to tell the landlord or property manager first, and in an emergency you can shut off the water and call both.

Do you work on apartments near Heritage Park?

Call and describe the building and the problem. Work on shared lines in a larger building usually needs the property manager's approval first.

Do you serve homes that are near the park but not right next to it?

Yes. We serve all of Cerritos, so a call does not depend on how close you are to the park.
