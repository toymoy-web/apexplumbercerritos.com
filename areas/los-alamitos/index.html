Rossmoor-Adjacent Coverage

We take calls from the neighboring Rossmoor community, which shares the same 90720 ZIP code and housing era as Los Alamitos proper.

Los Alamitos incorporated as a city in 1960, the same decade most of Cerritos was built out. Its residential character was shaped by the neighboring Rossmoor tract developments: Dutch Haven in 1960, Rossmoor Highlands in 1961, and College Park North in 1967. That shared construction era means the same original galvanized and copper supply lines common in Cerritos homes show up in Los Alamitos as well.

A large share of the city's land is occupied by the Joint Forces Training Base, so residential coverage is concentrated in a compact area, which keeps response times short across town. That compactness also means we get to know the housing stock in a small city more thoroughly than in a sprawling one. The Los Alamitos Race Course, despite carrying the city's name, actually sits just over the line in neighboring Cypress, which is a useful distinction when confirming a service address falls inside our coverage area.

At around 12,000 residents, Los Alamitos is the smallest city in our coverage area, and its single ZIP code makes it one of the simplest to schedule same-day appointments in regardless of which street a call comes from within city limits.
