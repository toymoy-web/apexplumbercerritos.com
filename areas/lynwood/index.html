Lynwood incorporated in 1921, making it older than Cerritos and most of the other cities in this network, and it sits in Los Angeles County rather than Orange County. It's also one of the more densely populated cities on our routes, with a large share of multi-family and closely spaced single-family housing built well before the 1960s tract boom that shaped Cerritos.

That combination of older housing stock and higher density means shared drain and sewer lines see more total use per line, which is a common factor behind the recurring clogs and backups we're called out for in Lynwood. It also means a single plumbing issue is more likely to affect a neighboring unit, which changes how urgently a call gets scheduled. The city itself is named after the old Lynwood Dairy and Creamery, whose owner's wife, Lynn Wood Sessions, gave the area its name well before the railroad station and surrounding subdivision took it on.

Lynwood is one of the most densely populated cities in this coverage area, packed into under five square miles, which is part of why we treat a shared-line diagnosis as the default question to rule out first rather than an afterthought. Its single ZIP code, 90262, keeps routing simple even though the call volume per square mile runs higher than in our other coverage cities.
