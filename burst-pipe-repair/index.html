In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

A burst pipe in Cerritos is most often a copper line that's finally failed after decades of hard water and, in some cases, seasonal soil movement stressing a joint. Original 1960s and 1970s copper is well past its typical service life in a lot of the city, which is why a sudden burst, rather than a slow leak, sometimes comes as a surprise even in a home with no prior plumbing issues.

What To Do Right Now.

If You Have An Active Burst Pipe.

Shut Off The Main Valve

Locate your home's main water shutoff, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and turn it fully off.

Shut Off Power Near Standing Water

If water is pooling near an electrical outlet or panel, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Move Valuables Away From The Water

Where it's safe to do so, move furniture, electronics, and belongings away from the affected area while help is on the way.

Call Rather Than Wait

A burst pipe gets worse the longer it runs. Calling immediately, even before you've located the shutoff, gets a technician moving toward you sooner rather than losing valuable time.
