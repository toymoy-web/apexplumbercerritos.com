In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Cerritos homes built in the 1960s and 1970s often still run original cast-iron or clay drain lines, and both are more prone to scale buildup and root intrusion than modern PVC or ABS pipe. That age difference is a big part of why a drain that clogs once in a newer home might clog repeatedly in an older one, and why we check the line's material and age before deciding between cabling and hydro jetting. It also affects how often we recommend a camera inspection instead of just clearing the immediate clog and moving on.

Common Signs

Signs Your Drain Needs More Than A Plunger

Water Backs Up, Not Just Slows

If water rises in a second fixture when you run another one, the clog is past the trap and further down the shared line, which a plunger can't reach.

It Clogs Again Within Weeks

A drain that was snaked recently and clogged again usually has grease buildup or root intrusion the cable didn't fully clear, which is where hydro jetting helps.

Gurgling From Other Drains

A gurgling toilet or tub when the washing machine drains is a sign of a partial blockage in the main line, not just one fixture.

Slow Kitchen Sink After Dishes

Grease and food particles building up in a kitchen line is one of the most common calls we get in Cerritos kitchens, especially in older homes.

Cabling vs. Hydro Jetting.

Which Method Your Drain Needs.

Drain Cabling

  • Best for a single, localized clog.
  • Faster for hair and small object jams.
  • Lower cost per visit.
  • Doesn't fully clear pipe walls.

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears the full diameter of the pipe.
  • Removes grease buildup and root intrusion.
  • Better for drains that clog repeatedly.
  • Longer-lasting result on main lines.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you clear a main sewer line, not just a sink?

Yes. We cable and hydro jet main lines, not only fixture drains, and can camera-inspect the line if it keeps clogging.

Will hydro jetting damage old pipe?

We check pipe condition first. Hydro jetting is safe on sound pipe but we won't run high pressure through a line that's already cracked or collapsing.

How fast can someone come out?

Most drain calls in Cerritos get a same-day appointment when you call before mid-afternoon.

Is hydro jetting worth it over just snaking?

For a drain that keeps coming back, yes. Snaking punches a hole through the clog, while jetting clears the full width of the pipe and removes what caused it.

Is your drain and sewer work up to code?

Yes. All drain and sewer line work follows California Plumbing Code standards for pipe materials, slope, and connections.
