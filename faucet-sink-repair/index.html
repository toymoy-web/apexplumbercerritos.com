In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Faucet and valve calls are one of the higher-volume services we get across our whole coverage area, and Cerritos' hard water plays a real role in why. Mineral buildup inside a faucet cartridge or shutoff valve is a common cause of a fixture that drips, won't fully shut off, or becomes stiff to operate, especially in homes with original 1960s and 1970s fixtures that have never been serviced. Replacing an aging shutoff valve during an unrelated repair is a common recommendation, since a valve that fails during an actual emergency is far more disruptive than one replaced ahead of time.

Common Signs

Signs Your Faucet Or Valve Needs Attention

Persistent Dripping

A faucet that keeps dripping after the handle is off usually has a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring inside.

Valve Won't Fully Shut Off

A shutoff valve that keeps leaking after being closed is a real problem if you ever need to isolate that fixture quickly.

Leak Behind The Wall Or Under The Sink

Water appearing inside a cabinet or on the wall behind a fixture often traces back to a failing supply line or valve connection that's easy to miss until it's already caused damage.

Low Or Inconsistent Water Pressure At One Fixture

When only one faucet has weak pressure, mineral buildup inside that specific fixture's cartridge is a common cause.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you match my existing faucet brand?

In many cases, yes. We carry or can source parts for most major brands, and we'll let you know if an exact match isn't practical.

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing or should I replace it?

If the body of the faucet is otherwise sound, a cartridge or washer repair is usually the more cost-effective fix.

Why won't my shutoff valve stop the water completely?

Mineral buildup or a worn valve seat are the most common causes, and both are usually repairable without replacing the entire valve assembly.

Do you install new sinks during a remodel?

Yes, including reconnecting the disposal, drain assembly, and dishwasher line affected by the sink swap.

How long does a typical faucet repair take?

Most faucet and valve repairs are finished within an hour once the technician is on site and has diagnosed the issue accurately.

Is faucet and valve work covered by a specific code?

Yes. Fixture and valve installation follows California Plumbing Code standards for connections and backflow protection.
