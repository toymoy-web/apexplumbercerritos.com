Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Gas line calls in Cerritos often come up alongside another project, most commonly a tankless water heater conversion that needs additional gas capacity, or a new outdoor kitchen or fire feature being added to a backyard. Original gas lines in 1960s and 1970s homes were sized for the appliances of that era, which sometimes means a capacity upgrade is needed for modern demand.

When To Call Immediately.

If You Smell Gas Or Suspect A Leak.

Rotten Egg Smell Near An Appliance Or Line

Leave the area and call from outside the home. Do not operate light switches, appliances, or anything that could create a spark.

Hissing Sound Near A Gas Connection

An audible hissing near a gas line or appliance connection is a sign of active leakage and should be treated as urgent.

Dead Or Dying Vegetation Near A Buried Line

Unexplained plant dieback in a line along a buried gas line's path can indicate a slow underground leak.

An Appliance Not Igniting Properly

Inconsistent ignition on a gas appliance can point to a supply issue worth having checked, even without an obvious smell.

If You Suspect A Leak Right Now.

What To Do Before We Arrive.

If you smell gas, leave the house immediately and call from a safe distance, ideally from outside. Do not attempt to locate the leak yourself, operate any switches or appliances, or use a lighter or match to check for it. If you know where your gas meter's shutoff valve is and can reach it safely from outside, shutting it off is reasonable; otherwise, wait for a technician or your gas utility's emergency line.

FAQs

Common Questions

What should I do if I smell gas in my house?

Leave immediately, don't operate any switches or appliances, and call from a safe distance outside. If you can safely reach your meter's shutoff, turning it off is reasonable.

Can you add a gas line for an outdoor kitchen?

Yes, including sizing the line correctly for the appliances planned and running it to the new location.

Does my current gas line support a tankless water heater?

It depends on the line's existing capacity and the unit's demand. We assess this before any tankless conversion to confirm or upgrade as needed.

Is gas line work up to code?

Yes. All gas line installation and repair follows California Plumbing Code and applicable gas code standards for sizing and materials.
