Benefits.

Why Cerritos Homeowners Choose Us For Hydro Jetting.

Clears The Whole Pipe, Not Just A Path

A cable cuts a hole through a clog. Jetting scours the full diameter of the pipe, removing grease, scale, and root intrusion along the way.

Built For Repeat Clogs

If a drain or sewer line has been snaked before and clogged again within weeks, jetting addresses the buildup a cable typically leaves behind.

Safe On Sound Pipe

We check pipe condition first. Jetting is safe on solid PVC, ABS, or sound clay and cast iron, and we won't run high pressure through a line that's already compromised.

Works With A Camera Inspection

When it's useful, we pair jetting with a camera inspection so you can see the line before and after it's cleared.

In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Hydro jetting comes up most often in Cerritos on the same aging clay and cast-iron lines that drive our sewer line repair calls. City reporting shows a large share of the local water and sewer network is 40 to 60 years old, and root intrusion into that older pipe is one of the main reasons a line clogs again within weeks of being cabled. Jetting removes the buildup and roots along the entire wall of the pipe rather than just cutting a path through the center, which is why it holds up longer on these older lines than cabling alone.

What Usually Brings People To This Page.

If This Sounds Familiar, Jetting Is Probably The Fix.

"They Snaked It And It Got Stuck"

A cable that stops partway down the line, sometimes 50 or 60 feet in, is often hitting a mass of roots or scale buildup that a snake can push past but not clear.

A drain that keeps returning to the same problem after cabling usually still has grease or root material lining the pipe, which jetting removes.

"Root Intrusion In The Line"

Tree roots seeking moisture through small cracks in an older clay or cast-iron line is one of the most common reasons Cerritos properties need jetting.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipe?

We check pipe condition before jetting. It's safe on sound PVC, ABS, clay, or cast iron, but we won't jet a line that's already cracked or collapsing.

How is jetting different from just snaking again?

Snaking cuts a path through a clog. Jetting uses high-pressure water to clear the full diameter of the pipe, removing what caused the clog in the first place.

Do you jet commercial grease traps?

Yes. Restaurant and commercial kitchen lines are a regular part of our jetting work, alongside residential sewer and drain lines.

Is jetting up to code for pipe cleaning in California?

Yes. Hydro jetting is a standard, code-recognized method for clearing drain and sewer lines under California Plumbing Code.
