Local Conditions

Why Cerritos Homes Need A Plumber Who Knows The Area

Original 1960s-70s Housing Stock

Cerritos was developed largely in the 1960s and 1970s, and homes from that era commonly still run original galvanized supply lines or aging copper. Both develop pinhole leaks and reduced water pressure with age, which is why a repipe estimate is one of our more common calls in the older sections of the city.

Aging City Sewer Infrastructure

City reporting shows roughly 70 percent of Cerritos' water pipelines went in between 1966 and 1974, with the broader water and sewer network now 40 to 60 years old. Older clay and cast-iron laterals in that age range are exactly what root intrusion and joint separation target, so a sewer camera inspection can catch a problem years before it becomes a backup.

Clay-Rich, Shifting Soil

Cerritos sits on clay, sandy loam, and adobe soils that expand and contract with the seasons. That movement stresses pipe joints beneath slab foundations over time, which is a common contributor to the slab leaks we get called out for.

Hard Water Mineral Buildup

Local water carries a moderate-to-high mineral load, which builds up inside water heater tanks and narrows pipe diameter over the years. That buildup is a leading reason older water heaters in Cerritos fail earlier than their rated lifespan.

What Cerritos Homeowners Call Us For

The Plumbing Problems We Hear Most

"I don't know where the leak is"

The most common call we get is a water bill that jumped, or a meter that keeps turning with every fixture off. That points to a hidden leak, and it is exactly what electronic leak detection equipment is built to pinpoint without tearing out a wall or a slab first.

"There's a warm spot on my floor"

A warm patch on the flooring or water showing up near the edge of a slab, often near the garage, is a classic slab leak sign. We locate it before recommending any concrete work, not after.

"It clogs again a few weeks later"

If a drain has already been snaked once and comes back, that usually means grease buildup or root intrusion further down the line than a cable can reach. Hydro jetting clears the full diameter of the pipe instead of just punching a hole through the clog.

"Do you actually have the equipment?"

This comes up on almost every leak and slab-leak call. Yes: our trucks carry acoustic and electronic leak-detection gear and sewer cameras, so we can show you where the problem is before any repair estimate is written.
