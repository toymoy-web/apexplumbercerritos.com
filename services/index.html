Before We Arrive

What To Do If You Have An Active Emergency

For a burst pipe, active water damage, or a suspected gas leak, shut off the relevant supply at the main valve or gas meter if you can safely reach it. Then call our emergency line right away. Avoid standing water near electrical outlets or panels, and don't attempt to investigate a gas smell yourself. We dispatch 24/7 for these situations.
