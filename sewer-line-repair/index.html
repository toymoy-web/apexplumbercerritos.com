In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

City reporting shows roughly 70 percent of Cerritos' water pipelines went in between 1966 and 1974, and the broader sewer network is now 40 to 60 years old in many areas. Clay and cast-iron laterals in that age range are exactly what tree roots and joint separation target, which is why a camera inspection before any repair matters as much here as the repair itself. The city has cited a recent overflow on Studebaker Road as an example of the strain that aging infrastructure puts on the system, and the same pattern shows up on private laterals throughout the city.

Common Signs

Signs Your Sewer Line Needs Attention

Multiple Drains Back Up Together

If the toilet, tub, and floor drain all back up around the same time, the problem is in the main line, not one fixture.

Sewer Smell In The Yard

A sewage smell near the yard or a soggy patch of lawn over the line often means a leak in the lateral itself.

Recurring Clogs After Snaking

A main line that clogs again a few weeks after being snaked usually has root intrusion further down than a cable reaches.

Gurgling Toilets

A toilet that gurgles when the washing machine drains is a sign of a partial blockage shared by both fixtures.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you need to dig up my whole yard?

Not always. A localized crack can often be spot-repaired, and trenchless methods can replace longer sections with minimal digging.

How do you know it's the sewer line and not just one drain?

Multiple fixtures backing up together, plus a camera inspection, confirms whether the problem is in the shared line.

Can tree roots really break a sewer pipe?

Yes. Roots seek moisture through even small cracks or joints, then grow and expand until they crack or block the pipe.

Is a sewer backup considered an emergency?

Yes. A backup affecting multiple fixtures gets priority scheduling since it can affect sanitation inside the home.

Do you handle sewer laterals on the city side, not just my property?

We work on the private lateral from the house to the city connection. Issues on the city-owned portion get referred to the city, and we can point you to who to contact and what to expect from that process.

Do sewer repairs need to meet a specific code?

Yes. Sewer line repairs and replacements follow California Plumbing Code standards for pipe material, slope, and connections.
