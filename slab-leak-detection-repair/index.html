In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Slab leaks are a real pattern in Cerritos because of the clay, sandy loam, and adobe soil under most of the city, which expands and contracts with the seasons and stresses pipe joints running through or beneath slab foundations. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, which make up much of Cerritos, commonly have copper supply lines running directly through the slab, and decades of that seasonal soil movement is a leading cause of the pinhole leaks we're called out to trace.

What Usually Brings People To This Page

If This Sounds Familiar, It's Probably A Slab Leak

"There's Water Coming Up Under My Floorboards"

Water pooling under flooring near a slab edge, often near the garage, is one of the clearest slab leak signs we see.

"There's A Warm Spot On The Floor"

A warm patch on tile or flooring, even without visible water, usually means a hot water line is leaking beneath the slab.

"The Meter Keeps Running With Everything Off"

If the meter dial still moves with every fixture in the house shut off, water is escaping somewhere in the system, and a slab leak is a common cause.

"I'm Having Problems Near The Edge Of The Slab By The Garage"

Leaks often surface at the perimeter of a slab, near a garage or foundation edge, before they're noticeable anywhere else in the house.

Repair Or Reroute.

How We Decide Which Fits Your Home.

Spot Repair

  • Single, localized leak point.
  • Smaller section of slab opened.
  • Lower cost than a full reroute.
  • Faster turnaround on most jobs.

Reroute

  • Multiple leaks or an aging line overall.
  • Avoids breaking through concrete.
  • Line run through attic or walls instead.
  • Often preferred for older copper systems.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you have to break my whole floor to find the leak?

No. Electronic and acoustic tracing locates the leak first, so any opening we make is limited to that specific spot.

Is a reroute better than a repair?

It depends on the line's overall age and condition. We'll walk through both options and their real costs once the leak is located.

Can a slab leak cause damage if I wait?

Yes. Ongoing water under a slab can affect flooring, foundation moisture levels, and the water bill, so earlier detection limits the damage.

Will homeowner's insurance cover a slab leak repair?

Coverage varies by policy. We can provide documentation of the leak and repair for your claim, but coverage itself is a question for your insurer.

Does a slab leak repair or reroute meet code?

Yes. Both approaches follow California Plumbing Code standards for pipe material and routing.
