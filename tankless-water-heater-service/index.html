In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Tankless units are more sensitive to Cerritos' moderate-to-high mineral water than a standard tank, because scale builds up inside the narrow heat exchanger rather than settling harmlessly at the bottom of a tank. Manufacturers generally recommend an annual flush in hard water areas like this one, and skipping that maintenance is one of the most common reasons a tankless unit starts throwing error codes or losing efficiency years before it should. Homes that switched from a tank to tankless in the last several years are a growing share of our service calls as those units reach their first real maintenance milestone.

Common Signs

Signs Your Tankless Unit Needs Service

Reduced Hot Water Flow

Scale buildup narrows the heat exchanger over time, which shows up as weaker hot water flow than the unit used to deliver.

Error Codes On The Display

Tankless units show specific codes for ignition, flow, and temperature problems, each pointing to a different likely cause.

"It's Been Years Since It Was Serviced"

A unit that's never been flushed in a hard water area is carrying scale buildup even if it hasn't caused a visible problem yet.

Slower Ignition Or Delayed Hot Water

A longer delay before hot water arrives can signal a struggling ignition system or scale restricting flow.

Annual Maintenance.

Why A Yearly Flush Matters Here.

In a moderate-to-high mineral water area like Cerritos, manufacturers of tankless units generally recommend a flush at least once a year to keep scale from building up in the heat exchanger. Skipping that maintenance is one of the most common reasons we see otherwise-healthy units losing efficiency or throwing codes well before the end of their expected lifespan.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless water heater be flushed?

In a hard water area like Cerritos, once a year is the general recommendation to prevent scale buildup in the heat exchanger.

What does an error code on my unit usually mean?

It depends on the code, but common causes include ignition issues, restricted water flow from scale, or a venting problem. We diagnose the specific code rather than guessing.

Is it worth repairing an older tankless unit?

Often yes, if the issue is a single part or scale buildup. We'll tell you honestly if a unit is past the point where repair makes sense.

Do you service all tankless brands?

We regularly service Rheem, Rinnai, and Navien units, among other major brands.

Can a tankless unit run out of hot water?

Not the way a tank can, but flow rate limits and scale buildup can make it feel that way, which is usually a service issue rather than a sizing problem.
