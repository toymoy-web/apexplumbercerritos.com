In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Filtration addresses taste, odor, and particulates, which is a different concern from the hardness a water softener handles, though the two are often discussed together in Cerritos given the area's water profile. Some households want a whole-home system, while others are mainly concerned with drinking and cooking water and are well served by a simpler under-sink unit at the kitchen tap. A remodel or new kitchen installation is a common time these conversations come up, since running a new line for a filtration system is simpler while other plumbing work is already underway.

Common Signs.

Signs Filtration Would Help.

Noticeable Chlorine Taste Or Smell

Municipal water treatment can leave a chlorine taste that a basic carbon filter addresses effectively.

Cloudy Or Sediment-Heavy Water

Fine particulates in water, especially after nearby utility work, are a common reason to consider a sediment filter.

Relying On Bottled Water For Drinking

If a household regularly buys bottled water over taste concerns, an under-sink filter often solves the same problem at a lower ongoing cost.

Wanting To Pair With A Softener

Households already considering a water softener sometimes want filtration addressed in the same project rather than as a separate call later.

Filtration Vs. Softening.

Two Different Systems For Two Different Problems.

A softener removes hardness minerals through a salt-based regeneration process. A filtration system removes taste, odor, and particulates through physical or carbon-based filtration, and doesn't address hardness at all. Some households benefit from both installed together, and we'll recommend based on what your water actually needs rather than bundling them by default. Testing your actual water, not assuming based on general area reputation, is the right starting point either way.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need whole-home filtration or just under-sink?

It depends on your concern. Taste and drinking water quality are often solved with an under-sink system, while broader water quality concerns call for whole-home treatment.

How often do filters need to be changed?

It varies by system and household water use, typically every three to twelve months, which we'll specify for your particular setup.

Can filtration and a water softener be installed together?

Yes, the two systems address different issues and are commonly installed side by side.

Is filtration installation up to code?

Yes. Installation follows California Plumbing Code standards for connections and backflow prevention.

Do you test my water before recommending a system?

Yes, we assess your specific water quality concerns before recommending a system size or filtration level, rather than defaulting to one option for every home in the area.
