Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Most tank water heaters last 10 to 15 years, and Cerritos' harder water tends to push units toward the shorter end of that range because of sediment buildup inside the tank. Many homes built in the 1960s and 1970s are already on their second or third water heater. For that reason, a straightforward tank swap is usually the right call, while homes looking to reduce long-term energy costs or reclaim closet space often ask about switching to tankless during a replacement. Garage-mounted units in particular tend to see faster sediment buildup here than in milder-water regions, which is worth factoring into a replace-versus-repeat-repair decision.

Common Signs

Signs You Need Replacement, Not Repair

Unit Is 10+ Years Old With Repeated Issues

Once a tank starts needing frequent repairs, replacement usually costs less over time than continuing to fix an aging unit.

Tank Is Leaking At The Body

A tank leaking from the body itself, not a fitting, means the tank has failed internally and can't be patched.

Rising Energy Bills From An Inefficient Unit

Older units lose efficiency as they age, and a new tank or tankless unit can noticeably lower water heating costs.

Running Out Of Hot Water Often

A tank that no longer meets household demand is a common reason to size up or switch to tankless for continuous hot water.

Tank Vs. Tankless.

Which Fits Your Home.

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront cost.
  • Simpler, faster installation.
  • Limited hot water supply per hour.
  • 10 to 15 year typical lifespan.

Tankless

  • Continuous hot water on demand.
  • Lower ongoing energy cost.
  • Compact, wall-mounted footprint.
  • 20+ year typical lifespan.

FAQs

Common Questions

How fast can you install a new water heater?

Most replacements are completed same-day for calls before early afternoon.

Do you haul away the old unit?

Yes, removal and disposal is included in every replacement.

Is switching to tankless worth the higher upfront cost?

For many households, yes, especially with heavy hot water use, but it depends on your gas line, venting, and how long you plan to stay in the home. We'll walk through the real numbers.

Is the price locked before work starts?

Yes, you approve a flat price before any installation work begins.

Do you handle the permit if my city requires one?

In cases where a permit is required for the installation, we can handle that as part of the job rather than leaving it entirely to you to sort out.

What size water heater does my household actually need?

It depends on household size and peak usage times, not just square footage. We size the replacement based on your actual hot water demand rather than a generic rule of thumb applied to every home.

Does a new water heater installation need to meet code?

Yes. Installation, venting, and seismic strapping all follow California Plumbing Code standards.
