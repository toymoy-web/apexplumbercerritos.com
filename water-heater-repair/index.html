In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

Local water in Cerritos carries a moderate-to-high mineral load, and that hard water builds up inside a tank faster than it would in an area with softer water. Sediment collecting at the bottom of the tank is one of the most common reasons a unit starts popping, heats more slowly, or fails years before its rated lifespan, which is why we check for buildup on almost every repair call. A water softener or filtration system can slow this process down, and we're happy to talk through that option after the immediate repair is handled.

Common Signs

Signs Your Water Heater Needs A Repair

No Hot Water Or Lukewarm Water

A dead heating element, a failed thermocouple, or a tripped gas valve are the most common causes of a unit that won't heat properly.

Rust-Colored Water

Discolored hot water usually points to internal tank corrosion, which is worth inspecting before it fails completely and floods the area around it.

Leaking Near The Base

A puddle at the bottom of the tank, sometimes near the edge of a garage slab, is a sign the tank itself is failing.

Popping Or Rumbling Noises

Sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank causes a popping sound as the water heats, common with Cerritos' harder water.

Repair Or Replace.

How We Decide.

Usually A Repair

  • Unit is under 8 years old.
  • Single failed part (element, valve, thermocouple).
  • Tank itself isn't leaking.
  • One-time sediment or pilot issue.

Usually A Replacement

  • Tank is leaking at the seams or base.
  • Unit is 10+ years old with repeated failures.
  • Repair cost approaches replacement cost.
  • Switching to tankless for space or efficiency.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you work on tankless water heaters?

Yes, including diagnosis, descaling, and repair for tankless systems from Rheem, Rinnai, and Navien.

Is a leaking tank always a replacement?

Usually yes. A tank leaking from the body itself, not a fitting, generally means the tank has failed internally and can't be patched.

How old is too old for a water heater?

Most tank units last 10 to 15 years. Past that, we'll tell you honestly if a repair is worth it or if replacement makes more sense.

Does hard water really shorten a water heater's life?

Yes. Mineral buildup insulates the heating element and settles at the bottom of the tank, making the unit work harder and wear out sooner.

Can I flush the tank myself to prevent buildup?

You can, but many Cerritos homeowners have us do it during a routine visit so we can also check the anode rod and valve condition at the same time.
