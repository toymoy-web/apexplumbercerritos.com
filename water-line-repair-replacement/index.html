In Cerritos.

Why This Comes Up So Often Here.

The main water service line, running from the city connection to the house, is often the original line installed when a home was built, which in much of Cerritos means the 1960s or 1970s. Combined with clay and adobe soil that shifts seasonally, that original line is a common source of the low-pressure and rising-bill calls we get, distinct from the interior supply lines a plumber checks first.

Common Signs.

Signs Your Water Line Needs Attention.

Low Pressure Throughout The House

Weak pressure at every fixture, not just one, often points to a restriction or leak in the main service line rather than a single fixture problem.

A Wet Spot Between The House And The Street

Standing water or an unusually green patch of lawn along the line's path is a classic sign of a service line leak.

Rising Water Bill With No Interior Leak Found

If interior fixtures and the meter test check out, the service line itself is the next place to look.

Discolored Water After A City Water Main Repair

Sediment disturbed during nearby utility work can temporarily affect water clarity, usually resolving on its own within a day or two.

Trenchless Vs. Traditional Replacement.

Which Approach Fits Your Line.

Trenchless Replacement

  • Minimal digging along the line's path.
  • Faster completion in most cases.
  • Less disruption to landscaping.
  • Works well for most standard runs.

Traditional Trenching

  • Full access to inspect the entire line.
  • Needed for unusual routing or obstacles.
  • Sometimes required by specific site conditions.
  • More disruptive but occasionally unavoidable.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need to replace the whole line, or can it be repaired?

A single, isolated leak can sometimes be spot-repaired. A line with multiple issues or that's reached the end of its service life is usually better replaced.

Will my yard be torn up for a replacement?

Not necessarily. Trenchless methods replace the line with minimal excavation in most cases, though some situations still call for traditional trenching.

Who's responsible for the portion of line under the street?

That portion is typically city-owned. We'll identify where the property line falls and point you to the right contact if the issue is on that side.

Is water line replacement work up to code?

Yes. Water line repair and replacement follows California Plumbing Code standards for pipe material, depth, and connections.

How long does a trenchless replacement typically take?

Most trenchless replacements are completed in a single day, depending on the line's length and how accessible both ends of it are.
